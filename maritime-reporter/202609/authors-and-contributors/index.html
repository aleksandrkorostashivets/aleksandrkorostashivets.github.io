Naoki Saito

Naoki Saito, General Manager, Ship Management Systems Department, ClassNK. Having attained a B.Sc. in navigation systems from Tokyo University of Mercantile Marine in 1995, Mr. Saito joined the National Institute for Sea Training where he provided on-board training for maritime cadets. He went on to secure an M.Sc. in maritime affairs in 2004 from the World Maritime University in Sweden. Mr. Saito joined ClassNK in 2013, taking a leading role in establishing its certification services of maritime education and training. Today, he takes responsibility for training matters relating to seafarers, alternative fuels, offshore wind, and the human element in the safety management system. In 2025, Mr. Saito led the development of ClassNK’s Prime Shipmanagement Guidelines.
