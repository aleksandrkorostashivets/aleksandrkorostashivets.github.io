Many companies conduct survey projects that involve sand reclamation, port or harbor expansion, offshore wind turbine and pipeline construction. The level of penetration and resolution needed, along with the water depth in the area, are key elements in determining which acoustic survey equipment is best for a project.

Why understanding the project requirements is key

In this “Sensor and Survey Solutions” video, Fred Hegg, VP Marketing, Sales and Business Development, discusses sensor and survey solutions for the oceanographic market, with particular emphasis on the extensive transducer capabilities and the proven success of the Aquapulse systems. He explains how FSI offers solutions for varying water depths and survey objectives, providing the optimal balance of penetration and resolution to meet specific application requirements.
