Every successful offshore project begins with critical questions.

What lies beneath the surface?

Are there buried hazards?

Has an asset changed since the last inspection?

As offshore projects become increasingly complex, answering those questions requires far more than a single dataset. Project teams must integrate multiple sources of information to make confident decisions in challenging underwater environments.

Hydrographic surveying provides the foundation for understanding the underwater environment. It defines the seafloor and provides the essential framework for offshore decision-making. But many of the questions that determine project success lie beyond what bathymetry alone can reveal. Buried infrastructure, unexploded ordnance, geological anomalies, asset condition, and low-visibility inspection require different sensing technologies that contribute unique perspectives.

Recognizing that no single sensing technology can answer every offshore question, OceanSight has assembled a growing network of specialized marine technology companies whose complementary capabilities help customers build a more complete understanding below the surface. The growing OceanSight portfolio currently includes Neptune Sonar, Ocean Floor Geophysics, and Sound Metrics.

Expanding the Underwater Picture

Today's hydrographic sonar systems rely on increasingly sophisticated acoustic technologies to deliver the precision, resolution, and reliability demanded by modern offshore operations. Neptune Sonar, a globally recognized OEM designer and manufacturer of high-performance acoustic transducers and arrays, develops acoustic technologies behind many of the industry’s leading sonar, positioning, navigation, and sensing systems. Working closely with equipment manufacturers, Neptune Sonar engineers application-specific solutions that continue to advance the performance, reliability, and accuracy of underwater acoustic sensing systems.

Building on the hydrographic foundation, Ocean Floor Geophysics (OFG) extends understanding beyond what is visible. Its line of patented self-compensating magnetometers, marine gradiometers, electromagnetic field (EMF) and non-contact cathodic protection measurement systems helps locate buried infrastructure, detect unexploded ordnance, assess asset integrity, and characterize subsurface features that conventional hydrographic surveys cannot reveal without towing. OFG's technology enables high-quality magnetic data to be collected directly from host platforms, such as AUVs, ROVs, USVs, ROTVs, or surface vessels, providing operational flexibility that traditional towed systems often cannot match.

When targets or areas of interest require closer examination, Sound Metrics acoustic imaging sonars deliver ultra high-resolution underwater visualization where optical cameras are limited by darkness, turbidity, or distance. Operators can inspect infrastructure, evaluate anomalies, and interpret complex underwater environments in real time, reducing uncertainty during inspection and intervention activities.

Each technology contributes a different layer of understanding. Together, they transform individual datasets into a richer, more actionable picture of the underwater environment.

The Value of Complementary Expertise

One of the most significant trends shaping marine technology is not simply the development of better sensors, but the ability to combine complementary datasets into better operational decisions.

When hydrographic survey data is integrated with advanced marine geophysics, high-resolution acoustic imaging, and the enabling acoustic technologies behind modern sensing systems, project teams gain greater confidence in planning, infrastructure assessment, hazard identification, inspection, and long-term asset management. The result is not simply more data, it's greater confidence in the key decisions made offshore.

This philosophy also defines how OceanSight continues to grow. Rather than assembling a portfolio of products, OceanSight is bringing together established marine technology companies with complementary technologies and expertise. As OceanSight expands through strategic acquisitions, each new addition strengthens the group, helping customers build a more complete understanding of the underwater environment.

Our goal is to bring together recognized specialists whose expertise naturally complement one another,” said Mike Read, CEO of OceanSight. When you combine those perspectives, you give customers a much more complete understanding of the underwater environment—and the confidence to make better decisions."

This is the future OceanSight is building—one company at a time.
